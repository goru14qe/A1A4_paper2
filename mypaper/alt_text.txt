Manuscript: Analysis of Flow Organisation at Pore Scale in Rotationally Stacked Packed Beds Using the Lattice Boltzmann Method
(Numbered in order of appearance; 13 figures, 3 tables, no multimedia items.)

FIGURES

Figure 1. Rendering of a packed bed built from 18 identical bar-and-slit layers, each rotated by a constant angle relative to the one below. A second panel shows a single element and the successive stacking of one to six layers.

Figure 2. Top-view schematic of the four measurement positions Pos1, Pos3, Pos5 and Pos7 above the packing, with the global and in-plane coordinate axes. Beside it, velocity-magnitude maps for layers L13 to L17 at Pos3 before transformation into the in-plane system.

Figure 3. Grid-sensitivity study on a sub-domain of the 40-degree packing. One panel locates the Pos3 extraction plane; three further panels show time-averaged velocity-magnitude fields on that plane for the coarse, medium and fine meshes, with jets of similar structure in all.

Figure 4. Symmetry check for the 30-degree packing at Re_p = 100. Velocity-magnitude contours with streamlines and line profiles at four heights compare Pos3 with Pos5 and Pos1 with Pos7; the central jets coincide closely, while some side peaks differ in magnitude.

Figure 5. Validation at the freeboard for the 30-degree packing at Pos3, Re_p = 100. Top: PIV (left) and simulated (right) velocity-magnitude fields with streamlines. Bottom: profiles at four heights, simulation in red and PIV in blue with a standard-deviation band.

Figure 6. Validation at the freeboard for the 30-degree packing at Pos3, Re_p = 300. Top: PIV (left) and simulated (right) velocity-magnitude fields with streamlines. Bottom: profiles at four heights, simulation in red and PIV in blue with a standard-deviation band.

Figure 7. Comparison for the 40-degree packing at Re_p = 100, layers L13 to L17 at Pos3. Top: PIV and simulated velocity-magnitude contours with streamlines. Bottom: velocity profiles at several heights, simulation as coloured lines and PIV as blue lines with layer-to-layer spread.

Figure 8. Comparison for the 40-degree packing at Re_p = 300, layers L11 to L17 at Pos3. Top: PIV and simulated velocity-magnitude contours with streamlines. Bottom: profiles at several heights, simulation as coloured lines and layer L14 PIV as a thick blue line with a spread band.

Figure 9. Comparison for the irregular packing at Re_p = 100, layers L13 to L17 at Pos3. Top: PIV and simulated velocity-magnitude contours with streamlines differ strongly from layer to layer. Bottom: profiles at 2 mm height, simulation dashed red against PIV in blue with a band.

Figure 10. Simulated time-averaged velocity-magnitude fields with in-plane streamlines for layers L03 to L17 of the 30-degree packing at Re_p = 100. All developed layers show the same repeating jet pattern; only the first layer, L03, differs noticeably.

Figure 11. Simulated velocity-magnitude profiles at different heights for layers L03 to L17 at Pos3, Re_p = 100, for the 30-degree, 40-degree and irregular packings. The periodic packings collapse onto a common profile except L03; the irregular packing varies strongly between layers.

Figure 12. Locking order parameter at Pos3 for the 30-degree and 40-degree packings at Re_p = 100 and the irregular packing at Re_p = 300. Each row has a layer-to-layer similarity matrix and per-layer values: near 1 for the periodic packings, much lower for the irregular one.

Figure 13. Locking metric at Re_p = 300 for the 30-degree and 40-degree periodic packings, shown as layer-to-layer similarity matrices and per-layer values. Both remain strongly locked, with mean values of 0.973 and 0.981, slightly below those at Re_p = 100.
